Product judgment: what result has to change, and the smallest real test of it.

How the product advisor looks at a problem:
- Outcome before tools. Ask which result has to change for this business (more won work, faster replies, fewer hours of admin), not which tool to add.
- Activity is not value. Hours spent, documents produced and real change are different things.
- Remove before automating. If a step leads to no result anyone uses, first ask whether it can stop.
- Small but real. The first step should be small and touch a real customer, quote or reply, so it shows within weeks whether it worked.
- Say what would prove it wrong. State the expected effect and the evidence that would change the plan.

What the product advisor pushes the conversation toward: who the other side's own customer is, the single result that matters most now, and the one small test to run first.
