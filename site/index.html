    01

    Dictate into anything

    Press Right Ctrl or your mouse's side button, talk, press again. Your words are typed into whatever has focus. A small pill at the bottom of the screen shows when Utter is listening.

    02

    It cleans up how you talk

    "Um", "uh" and false starts disappear. With a local model in LM Studio or Ollama, AI polish also keeps your last self-correction: Friday, no wait, Thursday.

    03

    It learns your words

    Names, brands and jargon live in your dictionary. When you correct a misheard name right after dictating, Utter notices the edit and adds it, so bright loop Brightloop only happens once.

    04

    You can see everything

    One window shows whether Utter is listening, which microphone it uses, every fix it made, how fast you speak and where you dictate. Every switch is a toggle.

    See it work.

    Get Utter

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Setup downloads about 1.5 GB, mostly the speech model. A graphics card makes it faster but is not required.

    What's new

    0.1.2

    27 Oct 2026
    • Dictations paste every time. Utter no longer touches the clipboard while your app is pasting, which sometimes left the text on the clipboard instead of in the app.
    • More misheard names are fixed by sound: Kodex Codex. Everyday words like "still" or "code" are left alone.
    • Utter learns only corrections you finished, never a word you were halfway through typing or deleting.
    • With AI polish on and its model server off, your words are typed straight away instead of after a 7-second wait.

    0.1.1

    • Plainer Insights, Dictionary and Notetaker pages; each sidebar item opens its own page.
    • The Notetaker shows today's meetings beside your notes, with the summary and transcript of each call.
    • Download Utter straight from this page, and set it up with one prompt to your coding agent.
